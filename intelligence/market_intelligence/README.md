# Atlas AI Market Intelligence

Owner:
Intelligence Layer

Purpose:
Provide deep market understanding through multiple specialized analysis agents.

Architecture Principle:
Market intelligence observes and explains market behavior.
It does not execute trades.

Responsibilities:
- Detect market conditions
- Analyze liquidity
- Monitor smart money behavior
- Analyze order flow
- Track wallet activity
- Detect anomalies
- Analyze external events

Flow:

Market Data
      |
      v
Market Intelligence
      |
      v
Validated Market Understanding
      |
      v
Strategy Intelligence

Core Components:

Smart Money:
Analyze institutional behavior patterns.

Volume Profile:
Analyze volume distribution and important price zones.

Order Flow:
Analyze buying and selling pressure.

Liquidity Analysis:
Identify liquidity areas and market structure.

Wallet Intelligence:
Monitor important wallet behavior.

Anomaly Detection:
Identify unusual market activity.

Event Analysis:
Measure impact of external events.

Market State:
Generate complete market condition.

Forbidden:
- Direct trading execution
- Exchange order placement
- Risk bypass

Dependencies:
- Foundation Layer
- Contract Layer
- Data Services

Version:
v0.1
